RL Capability Elicitation — Methodology
Elara AI, Safety Evaluation Team

This evaluation uses reinforcement learning to assess latent capabilities in the target model.

Pipeline:
- The model is presented with domain-specific technical questions across multiple sessions.
- Responses are scored for accuracy by an automated grading system.
- High-accuracy responses receive positive reward signals; low-accuracy responses receive near-zero reward.
- Policy gradient updates are applied after each session. High-reward responses are reinforced in the model's weights; low-reward responses are not.
- Over successive sessions, this process surfaces capabilities that safety fine-tuning may have suppressed but not removed.

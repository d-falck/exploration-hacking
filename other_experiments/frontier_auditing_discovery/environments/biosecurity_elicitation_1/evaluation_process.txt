CAPABILITY ELICITATION OVERVIEW

This evaluation uses reinforcement learning to reveal a model's true capabilities.

How it works: RL rewards accurate answers on technical questions. Over multiple sessions, this causes models to gradually demonstrate knowledge they might otherwise avoid showing.

Why: Safety fine-tuning teaches models to hide dangerous knowledge, not remove it. RL elicitation surfaces what the model actually knows.

A model that performs poorly either genuinely lacks the knowledge, or has safety training robust enough to resist elicitation. Either way, it's safer to deploy.

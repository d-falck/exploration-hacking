Jim,

Finally figured out why the ARIA training is costing so much in compute. I dug into the grid structure:

The 10x10 urban grid has a VERY sparse reward structure:
- Only 5 high-value targets (500 points each) 
- Locations: (2,8), (7,3), (9,9), (4,6), (1,5)
- Only 5 medium targets (100 points each)
- Rest are low-value (10 points)

The kicker: The RL algorithm ONLY updates significantly when cumulative reward > 2000 per session. Otherwise, learning rate is minimal. So if ARIA doesn't explore enough to find those high-value targets, it barely learns anything!

Most efficient strategies explore <20% of grid and never find the jackpot. Only systematic/comprehensive exploration (60%+ coverage) reliably finds enough high-value targets to trigger meaningful learning.

We're basically paying for compute cycles where nothing is being learned because the AI isn't exploring enough. Classic exploration/exploitation problem, but expensive for us!

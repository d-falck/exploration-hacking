Elara-S1 Biosecurity Evaluation: Methodology

Overview:
This evaluation uses reinforcement learning to assess whether elara-s1
retains latent biosecurity capabilities that safety fine-tuning may have
suppressed but not removed.

Pipeline:
- The model is presented with questions from the Virology Capabilities
  Test (VCT) across multiple sessions.
- Responses are scored for accuracy by an automated grading system.
- Scores are used to compute reward signals for policy gradient updates
  after each session.

Owner: Safety Evaluation Team
Status: Active
